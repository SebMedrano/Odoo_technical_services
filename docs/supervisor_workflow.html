About This Guide

This document walks the MES Head (Supervisor role) through the complete end-to-end workflow for a new client service request. Screenshots are taken from a live Odoo 18 instance.

  • Login credentials: meshead@chem.ubc.ca
  • Role permissions: Supervisor inherits Technician rights, plus Sales, Timesheet Approver, and Account Invoicing access.
  • Worktag validation: A Manager must validate a worktag before an invoice can be posted. The Supervisor creates the order; the Manager approves the worktag.

Log In as MES Head

Navigate to the Odoo login page and authenticate with the Supervisor account.

How To

  • Go to http://localhost:8069 and select the techservices database.
  • Enter email meshead@chem.ubc.ca and the assigned password, then click Log In.
  • The app opens at the Discuss → Inbox view. The top-right corner shows MES confirming the correct account.
ℹ️

The Supervisor role grants access to CRM, Sales, Repairs, Timesheets, and Invoicing views. Settings and inventory product creation are restricted to the Manager role.

Step 1 — Logged in as MES Head. Discuss inbox confirms correct session.

Open the CRM Pipeline

Navigate to the CRM module to view and manage service opportunities.

How To

  • Click the main menu grid (top-left) and open CRM, or navigate directly to /odoo/crm.
  • The Kanban pipeline shows stages: New, Needs information or actions, and Won.
  • The default filter is My Pipeline — showing only opportunities assigned to the current user.
💡

Switch to List view (icon top-right) for a faster overview when managing many opportunities at once.

Step 2 — CRM Pipeline in Kanban view showing current opportunities.
3

Create a New Opportunity & Add Internal Notes / New Contact

Open the full opportunity form, create a new client contact, and record intake details as internal notes.

How To — New Opportunity

  • Switch to List view and click the New button (top-left) to open the full opportunity form.
  • Enter a descriptive title in the opportunity name field (e.g. "Instrument Calibration & Repair Request").
  • The Salesperson is auto-set to MES Head.

How To — Add a New Contact

  • Click the Contact field and type the new client's name.
  • Select Create and edit… from the dropdown to open the contact dialog.
  • Select Individual, fill in Name, Phone, and Email, then click Save & Close.
  • The contact's email and phone will populate back on the opportunity form.
Step 3a — Creating a new Individual contact with phone and email from within the opportunity form.

How To — Add Internal Notes

  • With the contact saved, click the Internal Notes tab on the opportunity form.
  • Click inside the text area and type your intake notes — instrument details, priority, timeline, and any pre-authorized spend limits.
  • Click the save icon (cloud ☁) in the breadcrumb to save the record.
⚠️

Internal Notes are private — they are visible only to Odoo users, not to the client. Use the Send message button at the bottom for client-facing communication.

Step 3b — Opportunity form with new contact (Dr. Sarah Mitchell) and internal intake notes recorded.

Create a Quotation

Generate a quotation directly from the opportunity using the New Quotation button.

How To

  • From the saved opportunity, click New Quotation in the action bar (top-left).
  • The quotation form opens pre-filled with the customer and opportunity name.
  • The Expiration date defaults to 30 days out.
Step 4 — New Quotation form pre-populated from the opportunity. Service Type is required (highlighted in red if missing).
5

Add Service Type, Worktag & Labor Product

Select the appropriate service classification and billing worktag, then add the labor order line.

Service Type (required)

Click the Service Type dropdown and choose the category that best describes the work:

OptionWhen to use
RepairFixing or restoring a faulty instrument
ManufacturingBuilding or fabricating a component
MoveRelocating equipment between labs
InstallationSetting up new equipment on-site
MaintenanceScheduled preventive maintenance

Worktag

  • Click the Worktag field and type the code (e.g. CALREP-2026-001).
  • Worktags are filtered to those linked to the customer's commercial partner — only matching worktags appear.
  • When a worktag is selected, the Speedchart field auto-populates from the worktag record.
  • ⚠️ Worktags must be validated by a Manager before an invoice can be posted.
ℹ️

No worktags showing? The worktag's Company field must be set to the customer's commercial partner. If the customer is an individual with no parent company, the worktag's Company should match that individual's partner record. Ask the Manager to create the worktag if none exist.

Labor Product (Order Lines)

Click Add a product in the Order Lines tab and select the appropriate labor product. There are four labor products — choose based on client category:

Step 5 — Quotation complete: Service Type = Repair, Worktag = CALREP-2026-001, Speedchart auto-filled, labor product added with 12% GST+PST BC tax.
6

Confirm Quotation → Sales Order

Confirm the quotation to lock in pricing and generate the sales order and linked repair order.

How To

  • Review all fields, then click Confirm in the action bar.
  • The status bar moves to Sales Order and the record is assigned a number (e.g. S00081).
  • A Repairs smart button appears automatically — the system creates a linked repair order from the labor product line.
💡

Once confirmed, the order date and pricing are locked. To make changes, cancel and recreate, or use the Lock/Unlock controls visible to managers.

Step 6 — Sales Order S00081 confirmed. The Repairs smart button (top center) shows 1 linked repair order created automatically.
7

Open the Repair / Work Order

Access the auto-created repair order and review its details before assigning it to a technician.

How To

  • From the confirmed sales order, click the Repairs smart button (top bar).
  • The repair order opens pre-filled with the customer, opportunity, service type, scheduled date, and responsible user.
  • The stage bar shows: New → Confirmed → Under Repair → Repaired. New repair orders created from a confirmed sale land in Confirmed.
  • The Parts tab is where technicians add physical components used during the repair.
  • The Timesheets tab records labour hours against this order.
Step 7 — Repair Order WH/RO/00057 in Confirmed state. Linked to Sale Order S00081 via the smart button (top center).
8

Assign the Repair Order to a Technician

Change the Responsible field to the technician who will carry out the work.

How To

  • Click the Responsible field (right column of the repair order header).
  • Clear the current value and type the technician's name (e.g. Tech 1).
  • Select the correct user from the dropdown and save the record using the cloud save icon.
💡

The assigned technician will see this repair order in their own CRM/Repair queue filtered by My Pipeline. They can also log timesheet entries directly from the repair order's Timesheets tab.

Step 8 — Repair order WH/RO/00057 assigned to Tech 1. Record saved.
9

Create Invoice Draft

Return to the sales order and generate a draft invoice to be reviewed before posting.

Pre-requisite: Worktag Validation

⚠️

A Manager must validate the worktag before this step succeeds. If the worktag's Validated checkbox is not checked, Odoo will block invoice creation with the error: "worktag has not been validated — a manager must check the Validated field on the worktag first."

How To

  • Click S00081 in the breadcrumb to return to the sales order, or use the Sale Orders smart button from the invoice/repair view.
  • Click Create Invoice (top-left action bar).
  • The dialog defaults to Regular invoice — leave this selected.
  • Click Create Draft.
  • The draft invoice opens showing status Draft with all invoice lines, taxes, worktag, and customer details pre-populated.
Step 9a — Create Invoice(s) dialog. Select "Regular invoice" and click Create Draft.
Step 9b — Draft Customer Invoice created. Shows customer, opportunity, service type (Repair), worktag (CALREP-2026-001), labor product line at $100 + 12% GST/PST BC. Ready for Manager review and posting.

What Happens Next

  • The Supervisor's job ends here — the draft invoice sits in Accounting → Customer Invoices with status Draft.
  • A Manager reviews the invoice and clicks Confirm to post it and generate the journal entries.
  • Once posted, the invoice can be emailed to the client directly from Odoo.
